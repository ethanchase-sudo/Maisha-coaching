My story

The short version is: I've been where you are.

For twelve years I built and led people-teams at fast-growing companies. On paper I was thriving — promotions, a title I'd worked hard for, a calendar booked solid. In reality, I was the person everyone came to with their problems, and the person no one asked how she was doing.

It took a burnout I couldn't out-work for me to slow down long enough to ask a different question: not how do I keep up, but what do I actually want? A coach helped me find the answer. Then I spent the next three years training to become one.

Today I work with women in transition and the leaders they're becoming — founders, executives, creatives and caregivers who are ready to stop performing their lives and start living them.

"I don't believe you need to be fixed. I believe you need space, honest reflection, and someone in your corner who won't let you play small."

Mission

To help one thousand women lead lives that feel like theirs.

Every session, every resource and every program I create is built around one idea: that clarity is a form of freedom — and everyone deserves access to it.

That's why 10% of every program sold funds coaching scholarships for first-generation women leaders.

How I coach

Four things you can count on.

Honest, always.

I'll tell you what I see — kindly, clearly, and without sugar-coating. You deserve the truth more than you deserve comfort.

Whole-person, not just career.

Your work, relationships, body and rest are connected. We look at the full picture, because that's where lasting change lives.

Grounded in evidence.

Our work draws on positive psychology, the Enneagram and somatic practice — tools with depth, not trends.

Designed to stick.

Every session ends with a small, concrete practice. Insight is lovely; integration is the point.

Questions

Frequently asked

What's the difference between coaching and therapy?

Therapy often explores the past to heal; coaching focuses on the present and future to move forward. Many of my clients work with both. If something comes up that's better suited to a therapist, I'll tell you — and help you find one.

Do you work with clients outside the U.S.?

Yes. Most sessions happen over video, and I currently work with clients across North America, Europe and East Africa. Scheduling is shown in your local time zone.

Can my employer pay for coaching?

Often, yes. I provide invoices and a short ROI summary you can share with HR or your manager. Executive engagements are usually employer-sponsored.

What if I'm not sure which option fits?

Start with the intake form or a free 20-minute discovery call. I'll recommend the right path — even if that means pointing you elsewhere.
